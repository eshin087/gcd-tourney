Size fixes in the pack (ART-SIZE-R8)

13 strips that were drawn uniformly too big or too small, resized in the pack. The drawings are unchanged; every other frame of these fighters is byte for byte the same. Each row: the idle, then two frames before and after, all at the game's pixel density on one floor line (dashed line = the idle's crown; green background = after).

  • Sasuke: back air x0.93, forward smash x0.95, dash attack x1.06
  • Goku: forward smash x0.92, forward air x0.94, forward tilt x0.94, down tilt x0.93, up air x1.10, intro x1.10
  • Luffy: down special x0.82, forward air x0.88, heavy x1.12, forward special x1.08

Goku's back air could not grow (its kick already reaches the cell edge), so it is redrawn by Codex with the other 20 strips. Record: art/scale/size-r8/AUDIT.md.
