Clash HD effects

Eight clash effects matching each fighter’s approved HD move art.

Approved as drawn. The user approved all eight images via Claude relay beam-clash-6 #3 (“Approve all 8 (Recommended)”). Claude owns atlas/runtime integration; approval applies to these drawings.
Technical note: Generated sources were larger than square-cell strips. The five continuous bands were cropped, background-keyed, and palette-limited into exact 256×256 tiles. The Four-Tails source had white bars outside its magenta strip; those bars were removed from the derived tiles while the original versioned raw is preserved. C1 is imported from derived grayscale cells; Red and Dragon Fist are split from their preserved raw strips by the packer. Packer warnings for unrelated existing effects are recorded in the branch QA notes.
