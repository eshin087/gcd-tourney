Scale choices and production limits

The idle silhouettes including hair measure Naruto 214px, Sasuke 215px, Killua 187px and Gojo 244px. Gojo is 30.5% taller than Killua in this pose comparison; this is not a claim about exact upright anatomy. Hair, posture and bent knees affect visible height.

Every fighter uses one factor across all four poses. Sasuke’s source and scale are unchanged. Naruto’s body-length factor is also unchanged. The latest board uses 320px review cells so Gojo is not silently capped by the production cell; the previous round is placed on the same floor at its original native scale.

Weight is reflected in lean teen, lighter child and athletic adult builds; no kilogram values were guessed. These relative sizes are a visual proposal. A common cast-scale or cell solution must be settled with Claude before production packing.

All sixteen review poses have 48 colors per fighter and zero clipped pixels. These are static reference samples, not complete animation strips or a passed production scale gate. Previous hair-probe limitations and raw edge/palette caveats still apply.
