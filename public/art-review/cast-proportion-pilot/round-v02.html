Measurement and QA limits

The 256px review cells use one measured resize factor per fighter across all four poses. Each factor uses an inferred skull-crown-to-sole distance in idle. Crouching and hidden skull landmarks make literal standing height provisional. No pose was individually resized to fill its cell.

All sixteen review cells have 48 colors per fighter and no clipped pixels. Run is an airborne key pose; its lowest foot sits eight pixels above the floor. These samples are separate from the gameplay atlases.

The existing head probe reads Killua and Gojo consistently. Naruto's new gold differs from the production hair palette, so that probe cannot find his idle head. Sasuke's crossed dark sleeves join the hair-color region in guard and inflate its measured area. These diagnostics are recorded, not counted as a passed production scale gate. No gate exemption or art approval has been created.

Generated originals contain smooth edge and color variation. The review pack is quantized, but this does not establish full production pixel-art compliance. Approval here sets the anatomy direction; final strip packing and motion checks remain required.
