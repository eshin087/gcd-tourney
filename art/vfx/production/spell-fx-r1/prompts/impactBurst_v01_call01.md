# impactBurst v01 — call 1 prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: HD pixel-art VFX sprite sheet for a fighting game
Primary request: Draw exactly six sequential frames of a big-hit impact burst. Arrange the six equal square frames in a 3-column by 2-row contact sheet, ordered left-to-right across the first row, then left-to-right across the second row. The cells touch with no gutters, borders, frame numbers, or labels.
Scene/backdrop: One perfectly flat solid magenta #FF00FF background filling the whole sheet.
Subject: A compact bright core no wider than a fist at final 256×256 scale, with 6–8 sharp uneven spikes. Frame 0 shows the compact core and short spikes. Frames 1–2 extend the irregular spikes to full length, with a thin ring of force and a few small flying shards. Frames 3–5 break the spikes into fewer, smaller shards and sparks that fly outward and thin away. Keep the main burst centered at pixel (128,128) in every cell and keep all spikes and shards inside the cell.
Style/medium: Crisp hard-pixel HD game effect, visibly pixel clustered, shaded only in hard bands, white and two or three gray steps.
Composition/framing: Exactly six equal square cells, consistent scale, 3 columns × 2 rows, frame order left-to-right then next row, no cell gaps.
Color palette: White, light gray, medium gray, dark gray; flat magenta background only.
Materials/textures: Hard-edged pixel clusters with no soft rendering.
Constraints: Designed for a 256×256 VFX cell at 112 px per world unit, white-tinted by the game to white, blue, or orange. At most 16 colors. Keep the shape readable by silhouette, not brightness.
Avoid: Regular symmetric star, round filled disc, solid white disc, white-filled cell, black outline, blur, airbrush, antialiasing, gradients, dithering, noise, extra objects, text, labels, borders, watermark.
