# flare v01 — call 1 prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: HD pixel-art VFX sprite sheet for a fighting game
Primary request: Draw exactly four sequential frames of a small held light for a palm or beam root. Arrange the four equal square frames in a 2-column by 2-row contact sheet, ordered left-to-right across the first row, then left-to-right across the second row. The cells touch with no gutters, borders, frame numbers, or labels.
Scene/backdrop: One perfectly flat solid magenta #FF00FF background filling the whole sheet.
Subject: In every frame, a compact bright core about 24 px across at final 256×256 scale sits exactly at pixel center (128,128), with 4–6 short irregular rays extending no more than about 90 px. Vary ray lengths slightly from frame to frame for a seamless flickering loop. Add only two or three tiny sparkle specks around it. Keep the core compact and the surrounding area open; this is a small point light, not an expanding explosion.
Style/medium: Crisp hard-pixel HD game effect, visibly pixel clustered, shaded only in hard bands, white and two or three gray steps.
Composition/framing: Exactly four equal square cells, identical framing and scale, 2 columns × 2 rows, frame order left-to-right then next row.
Color palette: White, light gray, medium gray, dark gray; flat magenta background only.
Materials/textures: Hard-edged pixel clusters with no soft rendering.
Constraints: Designed for a 256×256 VFX cell at 112 px per world unit, white-tinted by the game to white, blue, or orange. At most 16 colors. The loop should flicker gently between frames.
Avoid: Any round halo, circle, bubble, concentric bands, solid white disc, white-filled cell, black outline, blur, airbrush, antialiasing, gradients, dithering, noise, extra objects, text, labels, borders, watermark.
