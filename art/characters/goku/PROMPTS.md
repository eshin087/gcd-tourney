## Source, measurement and scale record

- Design provenance: Dragon Ball Z, Cell saga, adult Super Saiyan in the orange gi worn against Cell. Goku KIT.md says about 175 cm; no underlying canon citation or uncertainty interval is present. Treat stature as approximate context, never a pixels/cm formula.
- Exact comparison images: public/art-review/title-style-pilot/{naruto,sasuke,killua,gojo}-neutral_v01.png. They are the only approved scale/style baselines used for v02. The Goku v01 file was attached to attempt 2 only as an identity/outfit/palette reference, never as a body-scale anchor. Held model/expression files and idle were not used.
- The lineup body comparison is neutral skull crown (hair excluded) to boot sole. Contract body targets are Naruto 195±3px, Sasuke 197±3px, Killua 176±3px, Gojo 222±3px; the contract's ±12 source-pixel crown landmark uncertainty converts to about ±3px at the pilot fit. Full silhouette bounds including hair are measured from the exact cells' pixel alpha: 205, 209, 186 and 236px respectively.
- Goku v02 neutral body is 207±4px; attempt 2 requested 205px, and the post-fit is 2px above prompt target, within measurement uncertainty. Fit uses the visible scalp/skull crown boundary under hair at source y=168±10px and sole edge y=980±1px (last sole pixel y=979), with one uniform fit for both poses. Full neutral silhouette is 234px including hair. Thus the body-only interval (203–211px) remains strictly above Naruto/Sasuke upper bounds (198/200px) and below Gojo's lower bound (219px). Combat is crouched, not a standing-height measurement. This is a visual relative-scale comparison, not calibrated anthropometry. No numeric anatomy pass is claimed.
- Final candidate: 512×256 PNG, exactly two native 256×256 cells. Original imagegen output was uniformly scaled with nearest-neighbor to the contract's common cell density, aligned on the boot baseline, composited on exact #FF00FF, and reduced to 48 colors without dithering. The lineup places the four unmodified exact approved cells plus Goku neutral/combat cells at the same density; it is review evidence, not a generated character candidate.
