# charge measurement interpretation erratum

The three f0 point ratios (.4536 torso, .3830 belt, .3705 boot) do not by themselves prove an incompatible scale. With target intervals of collar-to-belt 38.1±5 px, belt width 36±4 px, and boot top-to-sole 43.7±6 px, the ratios have a common overlap around .394–.421 before adding source endpoint uncertainty. Keep charge on technical HOLD for the observed loop snap and the lack of an independent comparable neutral frame; do not describe the ratio spread as an impossible-scale finding. No resampling was done.
