# R03 measured reassembly workflow

This procedure was used to produce Goku's exact final5 pixels. It separates source drawing review, scale calibration, packing, and runtime contact review.

1. **Inventory before editing.** Record every original source path, dimensions, SHA256, existing version, and any prior review state. Keep earlier versions immutable. Candidate reassemblies are zero-generation work and do not create a new design approval.
2. **Find the whole figure before using nominal cells.** Inspect the full-resolution source. Grid separators, a fist extending across a panel boundary, or an arm separated by a magenta gap can make a nominal crop incomplete. Mark verified separators and figure components; remove only a line or adjacent-frame fragment whose pixels are demonstrably not part of the figure. Preserve the uncut source and an annotated panel as evidence.
3. **Measure direct landmarks with uncertainty.** Use the approved idle v02 endpoints: collar center `(136,75)` to belt center `(127,112)`, `38.1 ± 5 px`; full belt span `(109,112)` to `(145,112)`, `36 ± 4 px`; rear boot shaft top `(70,197)` to sole `(58,239)`, `43.7 ± 4 px`. The title neutral/combat torso targets are `37` and `37.4 px`. Record source coordinates, panel origin, endpoint uncertainty, and reference uncertainty separately. Seek at least two stable segments. Do not treat a projected bent limb as an unforeshortened length, and do not average conflicting central ratios without interval analysis.
4. **Apply one measured factor to every frame in a strip.** Use the documented source segment(s), an explicit median or constrained interval method, and the resulting uncertainty. Never infer scale from total bounds, reuse a factor from a different source, normalize pose heights independently, or choose a factor just to fit 256 px.
5. **Preserve source pixels and grounded placement.** Use explicit panel rectangles when nominal grids split the subject. Keep every intact figure pixel. If a complete silhouette fits within the cell, measured integer `frameOffsets: [dx, 0]` may correct the packer's automatic horizontal centering; keep `dy: 0` for grounded frames. If full geometry cannot fit, record the uncropped extent and hold it rather than silently clipping or shrinking. The packer caps `bodyHeight` at 237 px; do not describe a clamped value as x1.
6. **Bind native height to actual x1 packing.** Set `bodyHeight` to the measured native maximum only after checking every frame's final `scale` and `frameScales`. Freeze the exact source, sidecar, manifest, pack settings, and atlas hashes.
7. **Make isolated fixtures faithful.** Copy the exact `art/scale/builds.json` and every referenced measurement cell/reference into the fixture. Record their SHA256 hashes and assert the expected `headProbe` mode, version-bound manual QA count, selected animation count, frame count, and x1 scales before interpreting a gate result. A fixture that omits calibration configuration is invalid evidence, not an art failure.
8. **Audit the final shared palette.** Run strict scale packing, extract every cell from the exact quantized sheet, compare all frames against atlas rectangles, and have Astra inspect every selected frame against idle and title neutral/combat. A previous palette's pass does not automatically transfer: rerun the visual review when atlas selection changes the shared palette. Report mask/alpha changes separately from RGB palette changes.
9. **Keep release decisions distinct.** Exact version-bound anatomy/scale PASS does not approve a new drawing or certify timing, gameplay contacts, floor grounding, hitboxes, production merge, or deployment. A technically held strip is excluded entirely; do not silently fall back to an older drawing that has its own unresolved technical findings. Ask for new art only when a source/action defect remains after extraction, uniform scaling, and valid placement have been exhausted.

## R03 fixture contract

- Audit baseline: approved idle v02 and approved title-style neutral/combat cells.
- Quantization: 48 colors; 256 × 256 cells; 16 columns; pivot `[128,240]`; `pixelsPerUnit=128`.
- Final selected output: `final5-fixture-public/characters/goku/sheet.png` and `anims.json`; SHA256 values and all93 frame records are in `manual-audit-r3/FINAL5_STRICT_AUDIT.md` and `FINAL5_PIXEL_VERIFICATION.json`.
- Strict scale pack: exit0, 21 selected animations including idle, 93 frames, all selected scales x1. `light3` and `heavy` are intentionally absent pending calibration. `getup` retains one floor/contact warning for Claude runtime review.
- The 20 selected non-idle versions are exact and version-bound in Astra's final audit. The two remaining holds are body-calibration decisions, not established missing-source defects and not an automatic request for new generation.
