Baseline: main commit `c83ab802`; the six `public/characters/<id>/sheet.png` and `anims.json` files in this checkout. The sheets are native 256px cells packed in the production palette. Versions and frame ranges are read from each atlas `anims.json` and its `scaleAudit` record.

Scope: every packed strip for Naruto, Sasuke, Killua, Gojo, Goku and Luffy, except Gojo `hurtHigh`, `hurtLow`, and `launched` per the inbox carve-out (PR #143 lane `gojo-run`). Counts: ok 207, height-pop 1, unresolved 21, scale-off 1; 230 strips total.

Method: visually checked every frame at 1x in one row per strip against idle f0. Naruto/Sasuke/Killua/Gojo sheets also include the approved title-style combat cell at the same density. Goku/Luffy are checked against their own idle only, as assigned. Body-area `B`, head-probe `H`, and bounding-box changes on crouched/airborne/lying poses are not treated as landmark results. `ok` means the visible comparable landmarks did not establish a repeated 5% shift; it is a visual screen, not a segment-measurement pass. `unresolved` means pose geometry hides a second homologous endpoint. For a `scale-off` call, two landmark dimensions and their native-cell coordinates are recorded.
